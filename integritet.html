Privacy policy

Livs-OS is a personal, self-hosted application with a single user: its owner, Pontus Senke. It accesses the owner's own Google Calendar to read and create events on his behalf.

  • Data from Google is processed only on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party.
  • Data is used only to show the owner his own calendar, warn about conflicts and add events he asks for.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Integritetspolicy

Livs-OS är en privat applikation med en enda användare, ägaren Pontus Senke. Den läser och skapar händelser i hans egen Google-kalender. Uppgifterna behandlas bara på hans egen dator och delas aldrig med någon annan.
